Example 110: Previously and Historically Combined
===================================================
Processes: P1, P2
Events: 6
Epsilon: default (infinity)
Expected verdict: SATISFIED

P1 initializes, becomes ready, then confirmed. P2 initializes and reports done.
The property "done -> (P ready & H safe)" requires that when "done" holds:
  1. "ready" was true at some point in the past (P ready)
  2. "safe" has always been true (H safe)

Every event carries the "safe" proposition, and P1 sets "ready" before P2
sets "done". In the linearization where P1's ready event precedes P2's done,
both conditions are met.

This tests combining P (Previously) and H (Historically) in a single formula
with two concurrent processes.
